City utilities

Parlier runs its own water and sewer

The City of Parlier supplies water from its own wells and treats its own sewage at a city wastewater plant, which received state revolving fund financing for an upgrade in 2021. That means water and sewer questions in Parlier go to the city, and Parlier Public Works lists a 24-hour number. Problems inside your property are plumbing jobs, and we handle them.

A backup that affects the street belongs to the city. A backup that stops at your property line belongs to us. Our drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning pages explain how we clear each.

Water quality

What Parlier's water report shows

The newest Parlier report on the city's site covers 2023. It lists hardness averaging 53 ppm, with results from 41 to 63, so the water is soft. It also lists 1,2,3-trichloropropane above the legal limit, and the city has treatment projects under way for several wells, with a housing-element note expecting completion of well work by December 2026.

Soft water means little scale on fixtures. Pipe age, pressure and ground movement still cause leaks, and leak detection finds them.

Around town

Neighborhoods and landmarks in Parlier

Parlier's housing element names the South Newmark and South Zediker neighborhoods and the Riverbottom neighborhood, which sits near the Kings River and carries flood risk. Parlier Unified School District's office is at 900 Newmark Avenue, and Heritage Park hosts city events. Major employers include the UC Davis Kearney Agricultural Center and a federal agricultural research center.

The 2020 Census counted 14,576 residents. The Riverbottom area is flagged for flood risk, so after a flood a sewer camera inspection is a sensible check on the line.

What we see

Why Parlier calls come in

Most Parlier calls fall into a few groups: a slow or backed-up drain, a water heater that has failed, a leak that shows up as a damp floor or a high bill, and a toilet that will not stop running. A short description of what you see lets the dispatcher send a plumber with the right parts on the first trip.

Codes and sources

Rules to know in Parlier

Plumbing in Parlier follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the UPC. Plumbing work is done under a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can look up any license on the CSLB website.
