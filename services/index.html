Clogs and backups

Drains and sewer lines

A single slow sink is a drain cleaning job. When the whole house backs up, sewer line cleaning opens the main line, and hydro jetting scours grease and scale from the pipe wall. A sewer camera inspection shows what is inside, and sewer line repair fixes cracked, rooted or collapsed pipe.

Hot water

Water heaters

A leaking or cold heater needs water heater repair, and a failed tank needs water heater replacement. Planned changes go through water heater installation, and on-demand units have their own page for tankless water heater service, including flushing in hard water.

Water lines

Leaks and pipes

Hidden water loss starts with leak detection. A leak under the floor is slab leak repair, a split or cracked pipe is burst pipe repair, and the buried pipe from the meter is covered by water line repair. Old pipe that keeps failing may call for repiping.

In the house

Fixtures and appliances

Overflowing or running toilets go to toilet repair and installation. Dripping taps and leaking valves are faucet and fixture repair. A jammed or leaking unit under the sink is garbage disposal repair, and new fixtures or remodel plumbing are plumbing installation.

Other work

Business and water quality

Restaurants, shops and offices use commercial plumbing, and homeowners who want to deal with scale can read about a water softener and filtration.
