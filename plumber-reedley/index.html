Around Reedley

Downtown, the college and the river: where Reedley's plumbing calls come from

Reedley grew around its downtown on 10th Street, where the city's Opera House at 1710 10th Street and the Reedley Museum at 1752 10th Street sit among older storefronts. Buildings that old carry old drain lines and supply pipe. A restaurant or shop downtown that has never had its line scoped is a candidate for a camera visit.

The city also has a wide residential spread, with Reedley College at 995 North Reed Avenue to the north and the Reedley Community Parkway crossing Manning and Reed Avenues. The Kings River runs along the edge of town, and the city's wastewater plant at 1295 West Olson sits on the river's west bank. The 2020 Census counted 25,227 residents.

Water quality

What Reedley's water means for your pipes

The City of Reedley runs its own groundwater system with wells numbered 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14. Its 2025 water quality report lists hardness averaging 93 ppm, with results from 26 to 160. That is moderately hard water, softer than Sanger's. The report also shows treatment in place for 1,2,3-trichloropropane, a groundwater contaminant that Reedley's wells have to treat.

For plumbing, moderate hardness still builds scale in water heaters over time. A yearly flush keeps a tank healthy. If you want your own number, test a cup of tap water, and ask the city's water division for the latest report.

Which services Reedley calls for first

Drain and sewer calls lead the list for most towns, and Reedley is no exception. A slow main line points to drain cleaning or sewer line cleaning, and a line that backs up repeatedly needs a sewer camera inspection.

Water heater failures follow close behind. When a tank leaks, emergency water heater replacement gets hot water back the same day when available. Hidden leaks and high bills go to leak detection.

Before we arrive in Reedley

If you have a burst pipe or active leak

Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house. Open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the pressure.

Keep power away from water

Stay clear of outlets and appliances in the wet area. Switch breakers off only if the panel is dry.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the way and photograph the damage for your insurer.
