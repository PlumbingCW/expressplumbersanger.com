About this area

Plumbing in Sanger's original townsite

Sanger was founded in 1888 after the Southern Pacific built its east side railroad, and it incorporated in 1911. The railroad depot, now the Sanger Depot Museum, is on 7th Street. The city has not published a boundary for the downtown neighborhood, so we describe it as the original core around 7th Street and do not draw lines.

The City's 2023 to 2031 Housing Element describes the original downtown core as an area with older housing and rehabilitation need. It also reports that 53.0 percent of homes citywide were built more than 30 years ago and 29.3 percent more than 50 years ago. The report does not give pipe materials for any street, so we do not guess at them.

Diagnosis first

What older construction means for a plumbing visit

Age alone does not tell you what is inside the walls. A home built decades ago may have been repiped, and another may still have its original lines. That is why we recommend looking before replacing anything.

A sewer camera inspection shows the real condition of the drain line, including cracks, roots and sags. A leak detection visit finds a hidden leak without opening walls blindly. Both give you facts to base a repair decision on.

Slow or repeat drains

Clearing the line with drain cleaning helps, and a camera shows why it keeps returning.

Rising water bills

A meter that moves with every fixture off points to a leak. Leak detection finds where.

Pinhole leaks or stained walls

Repeated repairs on one line may mean the whole run is worn. Repiping is an option once the cause is clear.

Heater past its prime

A tank that leaks cannot be patched. See water heater replacement.

Storefronts

Shops and restaurants near 7th Street

Sanger's Municipal Code requires grease, oil and sand interceptors where the waste calls for them, and it says the owner maintains the interceptor at the owner's expense. It also bars sewer discharge with more than 100 parts per million of fat, oil or grease. A food business that sees repeat backups should check its interceptor and its line before the next inspection.

Our commercial plumbing service covers storefront drains and fixtures, and hydro jetting clears grease buildup from drain lines when a cable will not.

Local facts

Water and sewer facts for Sanger

The City of Sanger runs its own water system from groundwater wells and operates its own sewer collection and treatment plants. Under the Municipal Code, the City owns and maintains the meter and its service pipe, while the property owner keeps their own service pipes, fixtures and stopcocks free of leaks. Septic tanks and cesspools are not allowed on premises that can reach a public sewer.

We could not find a published rule on who owns the sewer lateral, and we found no Sanger water heater permit exemption list. Ask the City of Sanger Building Division whether your job needs a permit before work begins.

Prep

Before the plumber arrives

Know your main valve

Find the main shutoff before trouble starts. In an older home it may be stiff, so turn it once to confirm it moves.

Pause the problem fixture

Stop running a draining sink or tub, and keep grease out of a storefront drain until the line is checked.

Look for patterns

Note whether one drain or the whole building is slow, and whether it clears then returns.

Call with the address

Give the street address near 7th Street, the building type and what you see.
