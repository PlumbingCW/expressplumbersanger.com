Sanger, ZIP 93657

Sanger: home base

Sanger is a city of about 26,600 people, according to the 2020 Census, and it sits east of Fresno in Fresno County. The city was incorporated in 1911 and calls itself the Nation's Christmas Tree City. Its ZIP code is 93657, and every service page on this site applies there first: drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection and the rest.

The Sanger Depot Museum occupies a depot built in 1887, and City Hall is on 7th Street. Sanger Park lies on Academy Avenue between 4th and 5th Streets, and Veterans Park is at Jensen and Indianola Avenues. The city's housing element names neighborhoods around JFK Park, Lincoln Park, Faller Park, the Washington Middle school area, Cherry Crossings and the original downtown core, and we dispatch to all of them.

Dispatch

How coverage works

One 24/7 line answers for every town. A dispatcher takes your address, the problem and any safety issues, then sends a licensed plumber. Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow, and emergency calls at night and on weekends are handled the same way as daytime calls.

Local conditions

What changes from town to town

Water chemistry, housing age and sewer systems differ across the valley. Sanger's city report for 2024 shows hardness averaging 145.5 ppm, which leaves scale on heaters and fixtures. Other towns buy or treat water in their own ways, so we suggest testing your own tap before buying a softener.

Housing age matters too. In Sanger, 53 percent of homes are more than 30 years old and about 29 percent are more than 50, according to the city's housing element. Older homes bring older drain lines, older shutoff valves and older water heaters, and those are what most of our calls involve.

Speed up dispatch

What to do before you call

  • Your street address and ZIP code.
  • A short description of what is happening.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you closed the main valve.
  • The age and fuel type of your water heater, if the call is about hot water.
  • A phone number we can reach during the visit.

The dispatcher uses your address to confirm the service area and send the nearest available plumber. If the problem is on the city's side of the connection, we tell you who to call.

Urgent calls

Emergencies across the valley

A burst pipe in Selma and a backed-up sewer in Reedley get the same treatment as one in Sanger. Close the main water valve if water is spreading, switch off the water heater if a hot line is the source, and call the 24/7 line. The dispatcher confirms your address and sends a licensed plumber.

If you smell gas at any address, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first. A plumber is for the water side of the problem.
