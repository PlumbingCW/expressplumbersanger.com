Urgent signs

When a clogged drain is an emergency

One slow bathroom sink can wait for a daytime visit. Several signs mean the blockage is in the main line, and those do not wait. Water backing up in a shower when the washing machine drains, a toilet that gurgles when you run the kitchen sink, or sewage showing in the lowest drain in the house all point to a blocked line shared by every fixture.

Same-day service matters here because a main line that is blocked keeps filling every time someone uses water. Our dispatchers book emergency drain cleaning for nights, weekends and holidays, and a licensed plumber works through the line with the right machine for the blockage.

Every drain is slow or gurgling

The blockage sits past the branch lines, usually in the main drain or the sewer lateral. Stop laundry and dishwashing and call for emergency service.

Sewage in the lowest drain

A floor drain, shower or tub at the lowest point of the house is the first place a main-line backup shows. Keep people and pets out of the wet area.

One fixture is blocked

A single sink, tub or toilet with all others draining normally is a branch-line clog. It usually clears in one visit with a cable machine.

Water rises when another fixture drains

Flushing a toilet and seeing the tub fill means the two share a blocked section of pipe. Do not keep flushing.

How we clear a drain: snaking, cabling and jetting

The method depends on what is in the pipe. A hand auger reaches a few feet into a sink trap arm. A motorized cable machine runs 50 to 100 feet of cable with a cutting head through a branch line or main drain, which handles hair, paper and soft roots. A hydro jetter scours the full pipe wall with high-pressure water and is the right tool for grease and scale. Our hydro jetting page explains when jetting beats cabling.

A cable pulls a hole through a clog. It does not always clean the wall, so grease can rebuild the blockage within months. Jetting costs more, and it cleans the pipe back to bare walls. A plumber should tell you which one fits your drain before the work starts.

Kitchen drains

Kitchen sink clogs and grease

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathrooms. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and hardens into a coat that narrows the opening a little with each wash. Coffee grounds and rice swell and pack into that coat. A kitchen drain that clears and then slows again within weeks is usually coated with grease instead of blocked by one object.

If a garbage disposal sits on that line, the disposal can also jam or leak at the same time. We check the drain line and the unit together, and our garbage disposal repair team handles the disposal itself when it needs work.

Local causes

Why drains clog in Sanger homes

Sanger's water comes entirely from city wells. The city's 2024 water quality report lists hardness averaging 145.5 ppm across its tests, with individual results as high as 330 ppm. Water that hard leaves mineral scale in pipes and turns soap into a curd that sticks to drain walls. Over years, that coating catches hair and lint in bathroom drains.

Housing age matters too. The city's housing element reports that 53 percent of Sanger homes were built more than 30 years ago and 29 percent more than 50 years ago. Older homes, including those in the original downtown core the city identifies as older housing, can carry cast iron or clay drain lines with rough, scaled walls that snag debris and let roots in at joints.

A blockage that keeps returning in an older home deserves a camera look, not another cable pass. Our sewer camera inspection shows whether the cause is roots, a belly in the line or a cracked section.

Drain line leaks and slow drains under the house

Some clogs hide a damaged drain pipe. Gurgling, a damp spot on the floor near a wall, a sour smell under a bathroom or a patch of grass that stays wet over the sewer line can all point to a drain line leak. Clearing the blockage restores flow, but a cracked pipe will keep leaking until it is repaired. If the camera shows damage, we explain the repair options on sewer line repair.

Before the plumber arrives

  • Stop using water in the house if several drains are backing up.
  • Do not mix or add chemical drain cleaners. They can burn skin and make the line unsafe to work on.
  • Move rugs and stored boxes away from the lowest drain in case of overflow.
  • Find the cleanout, often a capped pipe near the house, and clear access to it.
  • Take photos of any water or sewage on floors for your records.

Landlords and property managers

If you manage a rental in Sanger, tell the dispatcher the tenant's access hours and whether a cleanout exists. Repeat clogs in the same unit are worth documenting, because a camera report shows whether the cause is the pipe or tenant use.

Safety steps

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Stop running water into the blocked fixture.
  • Keep chemical drain openers out of the line, because they can splash on the plumber.
  • Do not use a plunger on a drain that is backing up with sewage.
  • Keep children and pets away from standing water.
Codes and sources

Standards for drain work

Drain lines are sized and vented under the UPC-based California Plumbing Code, which is why a repeat clog sometimes points to a venting or slope problem rather than to what went down the drain. Plumbing work is done under a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can look up any license on the CSLB website.
