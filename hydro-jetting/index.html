The method

What hydro jetting is and how it works

A hydro jetter feeds a hose with a specialized nozzle into the drain or cleanout. Water at high pressure shoots from jets on the nozzle, some facing back so the hose pulls itself forward, others spinning to scour the full circle of the pipe wall. The water breaks up grease, flushes sludge to the main and wears away scale and soft roots.

A cable machine opens a channel through the middle of a blockage. Jetting removes the buildup on the walls, so the pipe returns close to its full diameter. That is why jetting matters for lines that back up again a few months after a cable pass.

Urgent situations

When a backup needs a jetter right now

Call for emergency hydro jetting when a main line is full and a cable machine has not held. Signs include repeated backups in the lowest drain, floor drains that burble when any fixture runs, and standing water in a shower after laundry. Restaurants and shops with kitchen lines often need jetting after a grease blockage shuts a drain during service.

Because a full main line keeps filling, we book same-day and after-hours jetting visits when available. Our plumbers confirm the pipe can take the pressure before the nozzle goes in.

Grease buildup

Kitchen and restaurant lines coat with fat that hardens. Jetting scours it off the wall where a cable only punches through.

Mineral scale

Hard water leaves scale on pipe walls over years. Jetting scours it, which restores flow in lines that never quite drain fast.

Soft roots

Fine roots grow in at joints. Jetting clears roots in the line, but roots return unless the joint is repaired.

Repeat blockages

A line that has clogged twice in a year deserves a jet pass and a camera look to find the reason.

Jetting is not right for every pipe. High-pressure water can enlarge a crack or push through a thin, corroded cast iron wall. On older lines we run a camera first. If the camera shows a collapsed or badly cracked section, the answer is sewer line repair, not more water.

Local conditions

Why jetting helps in Sanger

Sanger's city wells produced hardness readings averaging 145.5 ppm in the city's 2024 water quality report, with some results above 300 ppm. Scale from water like that coats hot-water lines and thickens soap residue in drains. Jetting is one of the few methods that removes mineral buildup from a drain wall without taking the pipe out.

Restaurant kitchens anywhere in town face the other problem, grease. A restaurant line that has been cabled twice in a season is a candidate for scheduled jetting. Our commercial plumbing team can set a cleaning interval that fits your kitchen.

What affects the price of hydro jetting

Jetting costs more than a cable pass because the equipment is larger and the work covers the whole length of the line. We give you a clear price before work starts and explain what the camera found.

After the jet pass

Once the line is clear, the plumber runs the camera back through to confirm the pipe is clean and records any defects. If the cause was grease from a kitchen, the fix is a change in habits plus a cleaning interval. If the cause was roots, expect them to return unless the joint is sealed or the section is replaced. Codes and sources

Standards for jetting

Jetting is done with pressure and flow settings matched to pipe material and age, according to the equipment makers' guidelines. Older clay or cast iron lines get a camera look first, because cracked pipe can be damaged by high pressure.
