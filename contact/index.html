Phone

Call the 24/7 line

Call (855) 575-2890 any time. A dispatcher answers, and calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We do not use web forms, so there is nothing to fill out.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see, such as water on the floor, a backed-up drain or no hot water.
  • Whether you have shut off the main valve.
  • A good phone number in case we get cut off.
Address

Where we are

Sanger Express Plumbing, 2652 Jensen Ave, Sanger, CA 93657. Email: contact@expressplumbersanger.com. Email is for non-urgent questions. For anything wet or backed up, call.

24/7

Hours

The dispatch line answers around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow.

If it is an emergency

Before you call

  • Close the main water valve if water is spreading.
  • Switch off the water heater if a hot line is leaking.
  • Stay clear of wet electrical areas.
  • For a gas smell, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first.
  • Take photographs of any damage.
