About this area

The park and the neighborhood

John F. Kennedy Park is at North and Faller Avenues and has a splash pad and a playground. The City's 2023 to 2031 Housing Element lists a John F. Kennedy Park neighborhood, but it gives no boundary and no street list. We therefore say homes near the park, not a fixed set of blocks.

The same report says 53.0 percent of Sanger homes are more than 30 years old. It does not give the age of homes near this park, so we do not describe them.

Angle

Outdoor water: where a leak hides

A leak outdoors is easy to miss. A wet patch in a lawn, a hose bib that drips or a bill that climbs in summer can all point to a broken line.

Leak detection finds a buried leak with pressure tests and listening gear, and water line repair fixes the line once the spot is known.

Wet spot in the yard

Soft, green or muddy ground that stays wet may sit above a leaking supply line. Shut the main and see if it dries.

Hose bib that drips

A worn washer or a cracked fitting wastes water all day. A fixture repair usually covers it.

Bill that climbs

Check the meter with every fixture off. If it moves, call for leak detection.

Slow drains inside

A single slow drain is often a local clog, and many slow drains point to the main. See drain cleaning.

Local facts

Watering rules and backflow

Sanger limits when you can water. The City's web page and its Municipal Code list different days and hours, so check the City's current schedule before setting a timer. The City also runs a cross-connection program that requires backflow assemblies where needed, tested at least yearly at the user's expense.

If you plan to add or change an irrigation line, ask the City of Sanger whether a backflow assembly applies. We do the plumbing side of that work.
