Urgent situation

A failed tank is an emergency

A tank that leaks through the wall cannot be patched. The steel has corroded through, and the leak will grow. Water on the floor can ruin drywall and flooring in hours, so the first step is to stop the water and the fuel, and the second is a replacement.

We take emergency replacement calls 24/7, including nights and weekends, and book same-day installation when available. The plumber arrives, confirms the failure and quotes the replacement before removing the old unit.

  • Close the cold water valve on the pipe at the top of the tank.
  • Turn the gas control to off or switch the breaker off.
  • Open a hot faucet in the house to let the lines drain.
  • Mop the floor and move belongings away from the water.
  • Photograph the unit and the damage, including the serial label.

Choosing the right replacement

Standard tank

The most common type. Size by household. A 40 gallon tank suits one or two people, 50 gallons suits a typical family, and larger homes may need 75 gallons.

Tankless

Heats water on demand and takes less room. It needs a gas line and venting sized for the unit, and it needs a flush in hard water. See tankless service.

Hybrid heat pump

Uses a heat pump to cut electric use. It needs space for airflow and a drain for condensate.

Like for like

Swapping a gas tank for a gas tank in the same spot is the fastest route, often the same visit.

The work

What a replacement visit includes

1

Shut off and drain

The plumber closes water and fuel and drains the old tank.

2

Remove and set the new unit

The old tank is disconnected and hauled off. The new one goes on a drain pan if the location calls for it.

3

Connect, brace, test

Water, gas or power, venting, relief valve and drain piping are connected. The unit is braced, filled, checked for leaks and brought up to temperature.

California requires residential water heaters to be braced against earthquake movement, and the code sets standards for relief valve piping and venting. Permit rules vary by city, so ask the Sanger Building Division whether your replacement needs one. We never guess at permit rules.

Local conditions

Water in Sanger and what it does to a new heater

The city's 2024 water quality report lists Sanger hardness averaging 145.5 ppm. A heater in hard water collects scale, so a yearly flush helps a tank last. Some homeowners add a water softener to protect heaters and fixtures, which our water softener and filtration page covers.

If your old tank died young, check for a heavily scaled interior, a worn anode rod and high water pressure. A pressure check and an expansion tank, where needed, protect the new unit.
