Urgent situations

After-hours emergencies for businesses

Commercial emergencies run on a clock. A restaurant with a backed-up kitchen line cannot open. A shop with a flooded back room loses stock. An office with no water loses a workday. Our dispatchers take commercial calls 24/7 and book same-day visits when available.

Before you call, close the main valve if water is spreading, stop using the affected fixtures and clear access to floor drains, cleanouts and valves. Photograph damage for your insurer.

Grease traps and kitchen lines

A grease trap catches fats before they enter the sewer. When it fills, grease passes through and builds up in the line. Restaurants need regular trap cleaning and periodic line jetting. Skipping them leads to a backup during service, which is the worst time.

The City of Sanger operates the wastewater collection system and treatment plants. Ask Sanger Public Works about grease rules for your business, since local requirements can apply to food service.

Testing

Backflow testing and prevention

A backflow preventer stops contaminated water from flowing back into the drinking supply. Businesses with irrigation, fire lines, boilers or certain equipment can be required to have devices tested each year. Testing is done by a certified tester. Ask Sanger Public Works what applies to your property, and tell the dispatcher what devices you have.

Storm drains and parking lots

Sanger pipes stormwater to percolation basins, and the city notes that stormwater is not treated at its wastewater plant. That means anything washed into a storm drain stays out of the sewer system and goes to the ground. Commercial properties with catch basins should keep them clear of debris, grease and oil. We clean storm drain lines on request.

Honest note

Commercial leak detection

Commercial leak tracing takes more time than a house, because buildings have more lines, ceilings and tenants. Results depend on access. We run pressure tests and use listening and thermal tools. Pricing and scope vary, so describe the building when you call. For methods, see leak detection.

Local fit

Commercial buildings in Sanger

The city's housing element reports that 53 percent of Sanger's housing is more than 30 years old, and older commercial buildings carry the same age of plumbing. Cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines are common in older buildings. A camera inspection finds the weak spots before a backup does. See sewer camera inspection.

Sanger's hard water, averaging 145.5 ppm in the 2024 report, scales water heaters, coffee machines and ice makers. Scale protection is a practical cost saver for a business.

Codes and sources

Codes that apply to business plumbing

Commercial jobs follow the same plumbing code as homes, which in California is built on the UPC, plus local building department rules for tenant spaces. Plumbing work is done under a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can look up any license on the CSLB website.
