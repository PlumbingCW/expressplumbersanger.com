When a pipe bursts: the first ten minutes

Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise. It is usually near the meter, by the water heater or where the line enters the house.

Drain the lines

Open a faucet at the lowest point in the house to relieve pressure and drain what is left in the pipes.

Kill power to wet areas

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, keep clear and call for help. Switch breakers off only if the panel is dry.

Protect belongings

Lift furniture, roll up rugs, move electronics and photograph the damage before cleanup.

Then call the dispatch line. We book emergency burst pipe repair 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, with same-day visits when available. A licensed plumber isolates the failed section, repairs or replaces it and tests the system.

Causes

What makes a pipe burst or crack

Pipes fail from age, corrosion, high water pressure, a joint that gives way, a nail or screw driven into a line, or movement in the ground. Freezing is a cause in cold regions, but Sanger sits in the San Joaquin Valley, where the National Weather Service notes that Fresno sees lows at or under 32 degrees about 19 times a year and prolonged cold snaps are rare. Most local bursts come from age and pressure instead.

Exposed pipe on the north side of a house, hose bibs and unheated garage lines are the few places a night freeze can still cause trouble. Insulating them is cheap compared with a repair.

Common jobs

Copper pipe repair, cracked pipe and leaking joints

Many calls are not a spectacular burst. A pinhole in a copper line, a cracked fitting at a water heater or a pipe nicked by a screw can all leak enough to need immediate attention. The fix is a cut-out and replacement or a proper coupling, tested at full pressure.

Leaks inside walls need a small opening, usually in drywall. When the exact spot is not clear, leak detection narrows it. See leak detection.

After the repair: drying and insurance

Water that sat in drywall and insulation can grow mold in a few days, so dry the area quickly. A restoration company handles drying and removal of wet materials, and your insurer will tell you which services they cover. Photograph everything, keep receipts and keep the plumber's invoice, which describes the cause.

Safety steps

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Close the main water valve, usually near the street side of the house or at the meter.
  • Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and relieve pressure.
  • Switch off the water heater so it does not run dry.
  • Move belongings out of the wet area and keep out of rooms with wet outlets.
Codes and sources

Rules behind pipe repairs

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), sets the rules for pipe, fixture and water heater work in the state. Plumbing work is done under a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can look up any license on the CSLB website.

According to USA North 811, anyone planning to dig should call 811 at least two working days ahead so buried utilities are marked.
