About this area

A new community on Sanger's south edge

According to the builder, Somerset sits off North Avenue just east of Bethel Avenue, with models near Brehler Avenue. Local reports describe a plan for 490 homes in phases, with a 10-acre city sports park. Sanger's City Council voted 3 to 2 in 2023 to approve annexing 28 acres tied to the project.

SJV Homes also sells the nearby Blossom Hill community near Weston Avenue. We cannot confirm how many Somerset homes are finished today, so we do not state a count. Check with the builder for current availability.

What plumbing help a new home usually needs

A new home is usually under a builder warranty for its first years. If a pipe, valve or heater fails inside the warranty, start with the builder, since outside work can complicate a claim. Ask the builder what the warranty covers before you call anyone.

Once that is settled, most requests in a newer home are upgrades and fixture work. These are the jobs we hear about most.

Water quality

Sanger's water is pumped from groundwater wells and treated by the City. A water softener or filter is a homeowner choice, and the city's yearly report tells you what is in the water.

Garbage disposal and fixtures

Adding a garbage disposal or swapping a faucet is quick work in a newer kitchen.

Extra bathrooms or lines

Planned changes fall under plumbing installation, and the City decides what needs a permit.

Efficient hot water

Some owners add a tankless water heater in place of a tank. A plumber checks gas and venting first.

Water side

Who owns which pipe

Under Sanger's Municipal Code, the City owns and maintains the meter and its service pipe. The owner is responsible for the pipes, fixtures and stopcock on the property side. A leak on your side of the meter is yours to fix.

Because Somerset's annexation status is something we have not confirmed, ask the City of Sanger or the County about permits before you start work. We follow the California Plumbing Code on every job.
